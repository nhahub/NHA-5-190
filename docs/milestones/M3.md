# M3 - Compatibility and outfit recommendation

**Window:** October 4-10, 2026

**Status:** Planned contract; model, candidate/ranking outputs and downstream
acceptance are not verified in this repository.

## Goal

Generate structurally valid outfits, score compatibility, compute OCS, and return Top-K recommendations.

| Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| Eyad | Leakage-safe Polyvore examples mapped through the M2 cache |
| Hayat | Category, color, attribute/mask, and embedding interaction features |
| Ziad | Logistic-regression compatibility model and probabilities |
| Omar | Candidate generation and structural filtering |
| Hana | Pairwise scores, mean OCS, and Top-K ranking |
| Asmaa | Interpretable rule baseline, evaluation, and M3-to-M4 contract |

Candidate generation and structural rules stay separate from learned scoring so each can be tested and explained.

## Input contract

- Frozen M2 item schema and cache with approved dimensions, missing-value semantics
  and complete item-ID resolution.
- Polyvore outfit membership/compatibility examples and saved splits with
  product-level leakage evidence. The validation-only inventory and original
  outfit-position overlap check do not establish that evidence.
- Versioned structural rules and category policy agreed by the candidate owner.

## Output contract

- Traceable positive/negative examples and feature configuration; unresolved or
  excluded item references are counted and reported.
- Candidate records with item IDs, structural validity/rejection reasons, pairwise
  compatibility probabilities, OCS, model/config/cache versions, and Top-K ranks.
- Rule-baseline and logistic-regression evaluation with split identity and metric
  definitions; learned scoring is independently reproducible from candidate rules.
- Document the OCS aggregation, probability interpretation, allowed outfit structure,
  K, ties and missing-item behavior before freezing the M3-to-M4 schema.

## Acceptance

- Every returned item resolves to M2; emitted outfits satisfy approved structural
  rules and expose their scores and provenance.
- Exact fit/score/rank commands reproduce the reported populations and ranking
  under documented randomness and tie behavior.
- Owners report classification/ranking metrics against the agreed baseline and
  leakage-safe evaluation split; numerical targets remain a lead/evaluation decision.
- Hana and Asmaa review the output for M4 using the [handoff packet](../HANDOFFS.md).
  Model implementation and evaluation remain assigned team work.
