## Input contract

- Accepted M1-M4 handoff packets, frozen schemas/configurations, acquired data,
  saved splits, feature caches, models and integrated outputs.
- Approved metric definitions, evaluation populations, baseline choices and
  personalization/WUS scenarios. Sample checks do not replace full evaluation.

## Output contract

- Results by dataset/split/task with baseline comparisons, denominators, exclusions,
  runtime/hardware, leakage findings and relevant model/config/schema versions.
- Error analysis with traceable examples; generic/personalized comparisons and WUS
  sensitivity identify measured results separately from synthetic illustrations.
- Consolidated report, architecture/processing diagrams, presentation and a demo
  runbook listing exact setup, input acquisition, commands and expected outputs.
- A final artifact inventory with revisions/checksums and acquisition instructions
  for ignored data, models and caches; limitations and unresolved blockers remain visible.

## Acceptance

- Asmaa reproduces the integrated run from the owner's documented inputs and command;
  each evaluation owner signs off on their result scope and definitions.
- Report tables and demo outputs resolve to the same frozen revisions and configs;
  claims include measured evidence rather than assumed milestone completion.
- The team documents deviations, missing annotations/access and nondeterminism;
  dataset redistribution and code licensing decisions are recorded by the owner.
- Deliver the [handoff packet](../HANDOFFS.md) and final materials for project
  lead/instructor review. Their quality thresholds and final approval are human decisions.
